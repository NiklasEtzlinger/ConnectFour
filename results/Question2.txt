Move-count formula:
Connect Four has 7 columns. At the very start of the game every column is available (each has 6 empty rows), so every node in the search tree branches exactly 7 ways for the first several plies.

The search tree for depth d looks like this:

Level	Who calls it	Moves tried
1	calculateMove (root)	7
2	expandMinNode(d-1) × 7	7²
3	expandMaxNode(d-2) × 7²	7³
…	…	…
d	last alternating call	7^d
Every board.move() success increments moveCount once, so:


total = 7 + 7² + 7³ + … + 7^d
      = 7 · (7^d − 1) / (7 − 1)
      = (7^(d+1) − 7) / 6
Concrete values:

Depth	Formula	Count
1	(7² − 7) / 6	7
2	(7³ − 7) / 6	56
3	(7⁴ − 7) / 6	399
4	(7⁵ − 7) / 6	2,800
This is exact because at game start no column is full, so the branching factor is a constant 7 at every level — the sum is simply a geometric series with ratio 7.
